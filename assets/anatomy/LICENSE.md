## Changes made by FORGE

The source drawing is unchanged in `source/`. The generated images in
`front/` and `back/` modify it as follows (see
`scripts/anatomy/build.mjs`):

- recoloured to a dark palette (charcoal muscles, near-black tendons, bone
  and head);
- simulated directional lighting and edge shading added as a separate layer;
- front and back figures cropped and rasterised separately;
- named muscle regions extracted as separate highlight images in ember
  (primary) and copper (secondary), with a soft glow on primary regions;
- the gluteus maximus region clipped at the gluteal fold.

No anatomy was redrawn and nothing was generated by AI.

## Where the credit appears in the app

Every exercise detail screen ends with an **Anatomy illustration credits** link that shows the authors, the licence and links to the source and the licence (src/anatomy/credits.ts).

## Distribution

To keep the licence's share-alike terms, the adapted atlas (source drawing,
build script and generated images) is published in FORGE's public repository
under CC BY-SA 4.0. Whether that is enough for app-store distribution is an
open question (see docs/audits/PHASE_7_2C_3D_ANATOMY_QA.md, section 6):
Creative Commons' FAQ says material may not be shared on a platform that
applies DRM to it without the licensor's express permission.
